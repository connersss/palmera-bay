Vistamar is a top-down open-world crime game set in a sun-soaked coastal city. Walk the streets or steal a car and drive: traffic flows along the road grid, pedestrians stroll the sidewalks and scatter from fast cars, and a wanted level from 0 to 5 stars brings foot cops and police interceptors down on you. Take six story jobs from Mara Voss, earn fares driving a taxi, and burn off your heat at the Pay & Spray. A live HUD with a minimap and waypoint arrow keeps you on the job, and your cash and progress save in the browser.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser.
